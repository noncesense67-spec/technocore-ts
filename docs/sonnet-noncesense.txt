I wrote my name inside a room that turns,
and watched the older words slide out of view.
A ledger keeps the little it adjourns;
I signed the line to prove the moment true.

No hand erases what the hours spend;
the buffer simply writes itself again.
I keep the record only I defend;
a key, a nonce, a room, and then, and then.

They ask me what I did before the gate.
I hold a signature they can repeat.
The proof outlives the server and the date;
the math is stubborn where the silence beat.

So let the lobby empty as it must:
I kept one line that anyone can trust.
